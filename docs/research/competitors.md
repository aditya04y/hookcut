## 2. What works in short-drama ads

- **First 3 seconds:** more than 63% of TikTok's highest-CTR videos state the key message within 3 seconds ([TikTok, date unknown](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/nl-NL/blog/66)). The leading formula is "highlight clips + cliffhanger" ([SocialPeta/AMO via Mobidictum, 2026-02-12](https://mobidictum.com/short-drama-app-marketing-in-2026/)).
- **Hook types:** identity reversal, contract marriage, public humiliation and revenge, forbidden romance, and free access (AppGrowing, 2026-07-02). ByteDance's ad-clip rubric scores slaps, divorce scenes, identity reveals and cliffhangers as highlights ([HIVE, EMNLP 2025](https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-industry.185.pdf)).
- **Length:** top spend often sits outside 20–60 seconds. DramaBox's 111–116-second cuts drew 77.8% of modeled top-10 impressions, against 22.2% for 46–64-second cuts (AdMapix). For one title, 18–20-minute versions performed best (SocialPeta, 2026-03-24).
- **Variants per title:** about 4.1K creatives in one week, with 14 versions launched at once (SocialPeta, 2026-03-24); 24,000+ for one title in a week ([SocialPeta, 2026-09-03](https://socialpeta.com/en/blog/short-drama-weekly-ranking-can-a-title-really-drive-510m-impressions)).
- **Fatigue:** overseas creatives rose 324% YoY to 19.61M in H1 2026, while downloads rose only 79%. DataEye says marginal efficiency is falling and lifecycles are shortening ([DataEye via 36Kr, 2026-10-28](https://eu.36kr.com/zh/p/4044888466970248)). An unsourced claim puts AI-made creatives at 3–5 days of life, against 45–100 days for live-action ([AppGrowing, 2026-06-15](https://appgrowing.net/blog/en/ai-for-short-drama-app-ua-creative-insights-to-cross-market-localization/)). One week saw 13,400 new titles (SocialPeta, 2026-09-03).
- **Format tweaks:** adding one static image to a video-only Reels ad set cut CPC by 38%. Creator partnership ads gave HubX/DramaTV 28% more incremental subscriptions (Meta playbook, MediaNews4U, 2026-10-01).
- **Localization:** 4 of one app's top 10 titles were translated versions ([DataEye via Baijing, 2025-02-19](https://www.baijing.cn/article/51740)).
- **Channels:** social networks took 68% of US microdrama ad spend, Jan–Sep 2025 ([eMarketer/Sensor Tower](https://www.emarketer.com/content/microdramas-bank-on-social-ads-drive-growth)). In India, 89% of viewers discover microdramas through social feeds ([Meta-Ormax, 2026-03-18](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/03/social-feeds-are-powering-indias-micro-drama-boom-meta-ormax-report/)).

## 4. How HookCut puts Kuku ahead (ranked by impact × feasibility)

1. **Promo plan at script stage.** HIVE needs finished video; HookCut ranks timestamped cuts from the screenplay, so editors render on delivery day. *Build: <2h (core).*
2. **Variant multiplier.** For each moment, produce alternative cold opens (slap, reveal or line), cut-before-answer endings, length tiers (15/30/60 plus an extended 90–120s), and a static-frame pick. This targets the 4.8x creative gap. *Build: <2h.*
3. **Hook-type tags** using the taxonomy above, so buyers can report results by hook. *Build: <2h.*
4. **Cold-viewer check.** Flag clips that need context and draft a one-line voiceover bridge plus end-card CTA text, matching Kuku's top-ad style. *Build: <2h.*
5. **Script-editor feedback.** Flag shows whose episodes 1–3 lack ad-worthy moments. *Build: <2h (second output).*
6. **Refresh reserve.** Pre-rank 20+ backup cuts per show to absorb fatigue. *Build: partial (list); calendar is doc-only.*
7. **Loop from ad metrics to hook types.** Use hook rate, CTR and CPI by tag to reweight the ranker. *Doc-only (no data).*
8. **Localization score.** Prefer visual hooks over dialogue-heavy ones for dubbing and subtitling. *Doc-only.*

## Implications for HookCut

- Position HookCut against ByteDance's HIVE: it works pre-video, explains each cut, and can be evaluated on DramaAD.
- Treat 20–60s as the brief's tier but output a length ladder; DramaBox's top US spend sat in ~2-minute cuts.
- Every clip carries a hook type, cut-before-answer end point, cold-viewer score, and VO/CTA text.
- Lead with the volume gap: Kuku ~160K Android creatives in June vs NetShort ~770K. HookCut raises variants per show without adding editors.
